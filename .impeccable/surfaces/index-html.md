# Surface brief: Aschenthron (index.html, mobile-first game table)

Mode: Experience. Visitor is inside the game; the cards are the work.

Audience/job: a German-speaking player on a phone who wants a dark fantasy card game that is poker plus auto-battler, simple to read, deep to analyse.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole game is one hand-pulled print, a Totentanz broadsheet run in four pigments on charred paper. It refuses the glowing gold-on-black fantasy UI (runic rings, glow, fog, gold trim, round pills).

OWN-WORLD: Ground is Russ, a warm soot black with visible paper grain and burnt edges. Light ink is bone. Four flat pigments, each owned by a realm and never used as decoration: Safran (Sonnenwacht), Zinnober (Aschelande), Gruenspan (Nordmark), Krapp (Blutmoor). Card art is line-screen engraved in its realm pigment, with bone highlights. Type: Pirata One for names and headlines, Big Shoulders Display for stamped numerals and labels, Big Shoulders Text for reading, IM Fell English italic for flavor. Shapes are cut, never round: notched corners, hatch fills, registration marks and crop marks, off-register second-ink outlines instead of glow. Hard-edged square ash flecks and sparks. Primary action is a bone block with soot text; fold is Zinnober outline.

STORY: The player sees four seats and a printed table, understands instantly that the bottom third is theirs, bets or folds, sacrifices a card, builds a five-card team, and watches the engraved cards fight. Poker decisions stay readable (chance, price), the battle is cinematic.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title: two giant words ASCHEN / THRON in Pirata, the second word off-register in Zinnober; three engraved cards tilted like pasted prints dominate the lower two thirds; one bone button. Table: emblem plates of three opponents on top, printed board strip with crop marks and roman numerals in the middle, big stamped pot numeral, action row, and the player's two or three tall cards filling the bottom third.

FORM: Woodcut/engraved broadsheet (own grounded list, candidate 6, with the forging challenger's discipline of scale, hammer-hard impact and heavy stenciled numerals). seed: 4a42ce0e453b707d

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
